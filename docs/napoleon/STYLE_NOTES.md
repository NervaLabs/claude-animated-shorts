# Napoleon episode: how it follows the series style

Episode 2 reuses everything from `docs/alexander/STYLE_BREAKDOWN.md`:
- the 3D-set + 2D-card stickman look, ink outlines, lens blur, grade and vignette;
- word-synced cuts (each shot starts 2 frames before its word), camera glides, creep and kicks;
- the parchment map style with Cinzel labels, date stamps and label plaques;
- bold white word captions;
- the same narrator (Liam), the same −14 LUFS mix with music ducked under speech;
- the title card format: two big gold lines plus a small-caps subtitle.

## What is new in this episode
- **The hero.** Napoleon keeps one look throughout: bicorne with a tricolour cockade, dark blue coat with white lapels and gold epaulettes, white breeches, black boots. He uses the hand-in-waistcoat pose in calm moments.
  - Other versions of him: as a boy, as a young officer, in coronation robes with a laurel, and in a grey greatcoat for Russia and exile.
- **Period cast.**
  - Military: French line infantry, the Imperial Guard, redcoats, Prussians, Russians, Austrians, Mamluks.
  - Named figures: Wellington (black cockade), Blücher, the Pope, Louis XVI / XVIII, Joséphine.
  - Others: sans-culottes, scholars, cadets.
- **Sets.**
  - Corsica, Toulon, Elba and Saint Helena are one coast set.
  - Brienne school, with and without snow.
  - A Paris square and Notre-Dame.
  - One battlefield set in seven variants: Italy with the Arcole bridge, Egypt with the pyramids, Austerlitz in fog, Russia in snow, Moscow, Waterloo, plain.
  - A study with a wall map; Longwood House.
- **Weather.** 2D snow and rain layers over the 3D frame. The Austerlitz fog lifts into golden light when "the sun broke through".
- **Maps.** Europe 1789–1815: France, the Empire and client states of 1812, the coalition powers, routes (Italy, Egypt, Russia advance and retreat, Elba, Saint Helena), battle markers and glow points for the legacy beats.
- **Sound.**
  - Music: fife and field-snare marches; a regal D-major cue for the coronation; a slow winter cue for Russia.
  - Effects: cannon, musket volleys, fire crackle, rain, blizzard, organ and the death bell.
