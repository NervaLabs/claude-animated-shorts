# Fact-check notes on the 50 scripts

The narration uses your scripts exactly as written. A few of the claims are popular legends or are disputed. You may want to soften these before posting, for example "legend says..." or "reportedly...".

| # | Short | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kettle War | The soup-kettle detail is the traditional telling. Contemporary sources are thin. |
| 3 | Karánsebes | Widely considered exaggerated or legendary. The first detailed accounts appear decades later, and the "thousands of casualties" figure is disputed. |
| 6 | Wooden Bomb | Generally regarded as a wartime myth. No RAF record of it exists. |
| 21 | Caligula | From Suetonius. Some historians read "shells" (*musculi*) as siege huts, not seashells. |
| 26 | Cobra Effect | The Delhi bounty is anecdotal and undocumented. The well-documented case of the same effect is the Hanoi rat bounty (1902). |
| 29 | Yeltsin | Comes from one account (Taylor Branch, retelling Clinton). Never officially confirmed. |
| 39 | Abagnale | Many of his claims have since been challenged, notably in Alan Logan's research. |
| 42 | Mammoths | The Wrangel Island survivors are usually dated to about 4,000 years ago (about 2000 BC). 1650 BC is the late end of the estimates. |
| 44 | Parmentier | The guarded-field trick is a well-loved story, but partly embellished. |
| 45 | Nintendo / Ripper | The Ripper murders were in **1888**. Nintendo was founded in **September 1889**, so "the exact same year" is off by one. "The Eiffel Tower had just opened" (1889) is correct. |
